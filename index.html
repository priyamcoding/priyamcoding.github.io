I'm a cybersecurity professional and computer science student at the University of New Brunswick, graduating December 2026. With 2 years of hands-on SOC experience, I've protected thousands of users, reduced phishing click rates significantly, and led security migrations at enterprise scale.

My specific focus is healthcare cybersecurity - where the stakes couldn't be higher. Medical records are worth 250x more than credit card data on the dark web. Canadian hospitals have been paralysed by ransomware. Clinical staff are unknowingly exposing patient data through AI tools every single day. I want to be one of the people who fixes that.

But I'm also a speaker who has contributed to 600+ learners through Digital Skills for Women+, a conference chair who introduced girls in grades 7–12 to computer science, and a co-founder building cybersecurity solutions to protect local businesses & clinics in New Brunswick.
